Start low. This is the whole trick.

Begin at a third of the dose.

Almost everyone who decides fibre disagrees with them took a full scoop on day one. Your gut bacteria need a fortnight to adjust.

Build up over two weeks.

Add a third of a scoop every four or five days. If wind gets uncomfortable, hold at that level for a few more days rather than pushing through.

Drink more water than you think.

Soluble fibre pulls water in. Adding fibre without adding fluid is how people end up more constipated than when they started.
