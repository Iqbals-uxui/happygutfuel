What to take after antibiotics

The timing matters more than the product. One of these you can start on day one of the course; the others are wasted until it finishes.

Our pick Saccharomyces boulardii See why →
When to see a GP instead. Finish the full course your GP prescribed. Nothing on this page is a reason to stop antibiotics early, and probiotics are not suitable for everyone — if you are immunocompromised, ask your GP or pharmacist first.

During the course, or after it?

During → the yeast.

S. boulardii is a yeast, so the antibiotic doesn't kill it. It's the only one of these worth taking while you are still on the prescription.

After → the multi-strain.

Bacterial probiotics taken alongside antibiotics are largely wasted. Start once the course ends, and give it eight weeks.

Throughout → the fibre.

Feeding what survived matters as much as adding more. This is the cheap step almost everyone skips.
