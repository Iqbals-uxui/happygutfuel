Match the product to your pattern

Bloating after specific meals → enzymes.

If you can name the foods that set it off, the problem is digestion of that meal. Enzymes work on the meal in front of you.

Bloating most days → probiotic.

Background, everyday distension is more likely about what's living in there. That's a four-week project, not a same-day fix.

Cramping and spasm → peppermint oil.

If it's painful rather than just tight, enteric-coated peppermint has the longest track record and costs the least to try.
