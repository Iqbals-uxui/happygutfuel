About HappyGutFuel

A small UK site about gut health, written by one person, funded by affiliate commission, and reasonably strict about what it will claim.

Who writes this

HappyGutFuel is written by Sam Okafor. Sam is not a dietitian, a doctor or a nutritionist, and nothing here should be read as if he were. He is a writer who got irritated enough by supplement marketing to start reading the underlying papers, and who found that most gut-health content online is either a press release with a byline or a wall of citations that never tells you what to actually buy.

That matters more than it might sound. Gut health is a field where a lot of people with no qualifications imply they have some. If you want clinical advice about your own symptoms, see a GP or a registered dietitian — those are protected titles and the people holding them have done the training. What this site offers is a careful read of product labels and published research, and a clear account of where the evidence runs out.

How a product gets recommended

Every product on this site is checked against its actual retail listing first, and the write-up is built from what the listing says. Not the brand's own marketing site, not a press release, not a summary of other reviews. If a claim can't be traced to the listing or to a published paper, it doesn't appear.

In practice the things that get a product onto the shortlist are: named strains with their codes rather than a proprietary blend, a dose that matches what was used in research, a delivery format that makes sense for the ingredient, and a cost per serving that isn't silly. The things that get a product rejected are vague ingredient lists, doses well below anything studied, and health claims that aren't on the GB register.

What we won't say

  • That a supplement treats, cures or prevents any condition. It's not legal and it's not true.
  • That a probiotic will "colonise" your gut. Most strains are transient and leave within days.
  • That any product works for everyone. Response to fibre and fermented food varies enormously.
  • That we've tested something we haven't.

How we make money

Affiliate commission. When you buy through some of our links we receive a small cut from the retailer, at no extra cost to you. We don't sell placements, we don't take payment for a good review, and commission rate plays no part in what gets recommended or in what order. The affiliate disclosure page sets this out in full.

We're also developing a fibre product of our own, HappyGutFuel Daily. It doesn't exist yet. When it does, it will be labelled clearly as ours wherever it appears, and it won't quietly replace a recommendation that's currently pointing somewhere else.
