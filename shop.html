Best overall

Acacia Senegal Fibre Powder

Single-ingredient soluble fibre, unflavoured
Fibre/serv 6g
Type Soluble
Flavour None
Servings 60

The gentlest of the common fibres. Ferments slowly along the colon rather than all at once near the start, which is why it causes less wind than inulin at the same dose. Dissolves clear, so it disappears into anything.

Pros

  • Tolerated at doses that make inulin uncomfortable
  • No taste, mixes into any drink
  • Single ingredient, nothing to decode

Cons

  • Dearer per gram of fibre than inulin
  • Slower to show an effect

Chicory Root Inulin Powder

The cheapest way to add real fibre, and the one most likely to make you uncomfortable in week one. Ferments fast and early. Start at a third of the scoop and build up over a fortnight — most people who say fibre disagrees with them took a full dose on day one.

Pros

  • Lowest cost per gram of fibre by a distance
  • Slightly sweet, works in coffee
  • Large tub lasts months

Cons

  • Causes wind and bloating if you ramp too fast
  • Poorly tolerated by some people with IBS
