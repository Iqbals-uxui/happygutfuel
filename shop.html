Shop picks

Everything we currently recommend, in one place. If a product isn't here it's because we haven't looked at it properly yet, not because it's bad.

We don't rank by commission and we don't sell placements. Products are here because of what's on the label: named strains, sensible doses, and a cost per serving that isn't silly. Where something has a real drawback it's in the cons column, not buried in a footnote.

Probiotics

01

ProCombo Prebiotic & Probiotic — 200 Billion CFU/g

Synbiotic — probiotic plus prebiotic in a DuoCap™ double capsule
CFU/g 200 Billion
Strain La-14® L. acidophilus
Origin Made in France

The selling point here isn't the CFU number, it's the capsule. The probiotic strain sits inside an inner capsule and the prebiotic fructooligosaccharides are sealed in the outer one, so the two release at different stages rather than together. Both ingredients are named and patented, which is more transparency than most combination products manage. One capsule a day covers the adult dose.

Pros

  • DuoCap™ releases probiotic and prebiotic separately
  • Named patented strain and named patented prebiotic
  • Made in France by CAPSUGEL
  • GMO-free, preservative-free, lactose-free, gluten-free

Cons

  • A single strain — less diversity than multi-strain products
  • Most expensive per capsule of the three here
  • Some buyers report short best-before dates on arrival

02

Bettervits Probiotic Complex — 30 Billion CFU, 10 Strains

Multi-strain probiotic with chicory inulin and methylcobalamin B12
CFU 30 Billion
Strains 10
Origin Made in UK

Ten strains, plus 100mg of inulin from chicory root and B12 in the methylcobalamin form rather than the cheaper cyanocobalamin. UK-made, GMP certified and third-party tested. Worth a look mainly if you're already buying a separate B12, in which case this replaces two purchases with one. If you're not, you're paying for an ingredient you may not need.

Pros

  • Ten named strains for the money
  • Methylcobalamin B12, the more bioavailable form
  • UK-made, GMP certified, third-party tested
  • Vegan, Halal certified, free from gluten and dairy

Cons

  • Species names published but not strain codes
  • The B12 is irrelevant if you don't need it
  • Smaller brand with a thinner review history

03

Optibac Every Day — Daily Multi-Strain Probiotic

Daily probiotic with full Rosell® strain codes published
Storage Ambient
Strains Rosell® coded
Availability Wide

The one to start with if you have never taken a probiotic. Optibac publishes the full Rosell® strain codes, which sounds like a technicality until you try to look a product up. A code can be searched against published trials; "Lactobacillus blend" cannot. No refrigeration needed, and it is stocked in most UK pharmacies, so you are not locked to one supplier.

Pros

  • Full strain codes published, so claims are checkable
  • Shelf stable, no fridge required
  • Stocked widely, easy to reorder
  • Lowest cost per day of the three

Cons

  • No prebiotic included
  • Not targeted at any particular symptom
  • Fewer strains than the Bettervits product
