Our approach

Less hype. Better decisions.

Evidence first

We go to the research rather than repeating the claims printed on a product label, and we say when the evidence is thin.

Practicality counts

Ingredients matter, but so do dose, cost per serving, taste and whether anyone would realistically keep taking it.

Commercially independent

Affiliate relationships are disclosed. Being paid a commission doesn't get a product recommended, and we don't sell placements.

No miracle promises

Gut health is complicated and individual. We'd rather set out what's known, what's uncertain, and what's worth a try.
