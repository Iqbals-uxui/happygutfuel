Behind Nuptias

The Nuptias Story

Our story began in the autumn of 2015—two souls destined to be together and ready to embark on their new adventure! The beautiful dresses, the shopping, the jewellery, the make-up, the ring, the wedding favours… it all had to be perfect… every single little detail.

And of course, planning and preparing a wedding is lots of fun, right? Right? …Well, for a start, we didn’t have a clue where to begin. In all honesty, we were a little all over the place, trying to make everything perfect and find all the right things, at the right prices, in the right places… well, it definitely wasn’t easy! But we got there… eventually, alhamdulillah.

"After our wedding, we received so many enquiries about our bespoke, unique Zam Zam Water favours… it was right there that Nuptias UK was born."

We’ve tried to stay true to our core beliefs and deliver beautiful, elegant, and exceptionally designed bespoke Zam Zam favours for our community. We owe a huge thanks to each and every single one of you for joining us on this beautiful journey, and we hope that you’ll continue to be a part of our story and let us be a part of yours.
