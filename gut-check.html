The 60-second gut check

Seven questions about how you actually eat and what you already take. At the end you get a profile of where the gaps are and three things worth changing.

This isn't a diagnosis and there's no score. Nothing you answer leaves your browser. If you have persistent digestive symptoms, see your GP rather than a quiz.

Question 1 of 7

How many portions of vegetables, fruit, beans or pulses do you eat on a normal day?
Are your grains mostly wholegrain — wholemeal bread, oats, brown rice?
How many different plant foods do you eat in a typical week?
How often do you eat fermented foods — kefir, live yoghurt, kimchi, sauerkraut, miso?
Do you currently take a probiotic or a fibre supplement?
If you take a supplement, do you know which strains or which fibre is in it?
How's your sleep and movement most weeks?
