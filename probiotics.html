What to check on any probiotic label

A strain code, not just a species.

"Lactobacillus acidophilus" is a species — thousands of strains share it. La-14® or Rosell-52 is a specific organism you can search against trials.

A dose that matches the research.

CFU counts are marketing unless the number matches what a study actually used. Higher isn't better; matching is.

Whether it needs a fridge.

A product that needs refrigeration and sat in a warm van is a dead product. Shelf-stable formulations avoid the question.
